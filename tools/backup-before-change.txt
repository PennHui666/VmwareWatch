备份时间：2026-11-20 17:05:05
用途：把 VMware 4 个服务改手动、去掉托盘开机自启之前的原始状态记录

=== 服务原始状态 ===
VMAuthdService|StartMode=Auto|State=Stopped
VMnetDHCP|StartMode=Auto|State=Stopped
VMUSBArbService|StartMode=Auto|State=Stopped
VMware NAT Service|StartMode=Auto|State=Stopped

=== 开机自启项原始值 ===
[HKLM:\Software\WOW6432Node\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run]
vmware-tray.exe = "D:\VMware Workstation\vmware-tray.exe"

=== 回滚办法 ===
sc config VMAuthdService start= auto
sc config VMUSBArbService start= auto
sc config "VMware NAT Service" start= auto
sc config VMnetDHCP start= auto
reg add "HKLM\Software\WOW6432Node\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run" /v vmware-tray.exe /t REG_SZ /d ""D:\VMware Workstation\vmware-tray.exe"" /f
